VR Gallery

A VRChat photo album that knows which world each photo was taken in and who was there, from the logs VRChat throws away.

Download for Windows latest release

What it does

Who was there

Every photo shows its world, the avatar you wore and everyone in the instance when you took it.

Tag people

Press T and box a head. The people the logs say were there come first, so it's usually one click.

Years to days

Zoom out to find the stretch of time, then in on it. Each year and month shows its best photo.

Also: moments and sessions · people profiles · statistics and a year-in-review poster · cleanup of black shots and duplicates · Discord sharing · Quest import

Download

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Run Install VR Gallery.cmd, or just start VRGallery.exe from the folder.

Windows 10 / 11, no Python needed. Everything stays on your PC: photos are only read, and cleanup moves files to the Recycle Bin after asking. Leave it in the tray so VRChat's logs are kept before VRChat deletes them.
