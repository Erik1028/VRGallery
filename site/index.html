VR Gallery

A VRChat photo album that knows which world each photo was taken in and who was there, from the logs VRChat throws away.

Download for Windows latest release

What it does

Who was there

A photo shows its world, the instance type, the avatar you wore and everyone in the instance, wherever VRChat's logs or VRCX's metadata recorded them.

Tag people

Press T and box a head. The people the logs say were there come first, so it's usually one click.

Years to days, or all of it

Zoom out to find the stretch of time, then in on it; each year and month shows its best photo. All is the whole library as one sheet.

Also: moments and sessions · people profiles · star ratings, favourites and albums · statistics and a year-in-review poster · cleanup of black shots, bursts and duplicates · Discord sharing · Quest import · verified backup to another drive · XMP sidecars for Lightroom

Download

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Run Install VR Gallery.cmd. It copies the app to your user folder and adds Desktop and Start menu shortcuts. Or skip it and start VRGallery\VRGallery.exe right from the folder.

Windows 10 / 11, no Python needed. Your photos are only read; cleanup moves files to the Recycle Bin, after asking. Nothing leaves your PC unless you share a photo yourself; the app only asks GitHub whether there is a newer version. Leave it in the tray: VRChat keeps only its last few logs, and VR Gallery copies each session before they go.
